FAQ

What to know before your ride.

How do I request a transfer?

The quickest way is WhatsApp. Send pickup, destination, date and time, passenger count, luggage and your flight number for airport pickups. We then confirm price and availability.

Is the price fixed before the ride?

Yes. Website prices are starting prices. Your individual ride is confirmed with a fixed price before booking.

How does flight monitoring work?

For airport pickups you send us your flight number. We use it to take delays or earlier arrivals into account. Flight monitoring is included.

Do you operate early in the morning or at night?

Yes. Transfers can generally be requested around the clock, subject to availability.

Are child seats available?

Yes. Please tell us the child's age or the required seat type when requesting the ride.

Can larger groups travel together?

Yes. Our XL transfer can be requested for groups of up to 8 passengers and larger luggage volumes.
