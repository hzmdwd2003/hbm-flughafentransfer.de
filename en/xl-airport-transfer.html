XL Transfer FAQ

Common questions.

How many passengers can travel in the XL vehicle?

Up to 8 passengers are possible. Luggage volume also matters, so please provide it as accurately as possible in your request.

Can the vehicle take a lot of luggage?

The long passenger van provides substantially more space than a standard car. For very large luggage volumes, we check whether passengers and luggage fit together before confirming the ride.

Can I book XL for Frankfurt-Hahn Airport?

Yes. XL transfers can be requested for both Frankfurt Airport and Frankfurt-Hahn Airport.

Are child seats available?

Yes. Please tell us the child's age or required seat type when requesting your ride.

Can a group be picked up from the airport?

Yes. Send your flight number, passenger count and luggage. Flight monitoring is included for airport pickups.

Is the XL price fixed before the ride?

Yes. Once route, passengers and luggage have been checked, we confirm the price before booking.
