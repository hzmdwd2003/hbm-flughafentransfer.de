FAQ Frankfurt-Hahn

Common questions.

Do you drive directly from Frankfurt to Frankfurt-Hahn?

Yes. Direct transfers between Frankfurt and Frankfurt-Hahn Airport can be requested and are confirmed with a price in advance.

Can you pick me up from Hahn Airport?

Yes. Send your flight number, arrival time, passenger count and luggage so the pickup can be coordinated.

Do you also serve Mainz, Wiesbaden and Offenbach?

Yes. These are among the common routes for Frankfurt-Hahn transfers.

Can I request an XL vehicle?

Yes. XL transfers are available for groups up to 8 passengers and larger luggage volumes.
