FAQ Frankfurt Airport

Common questions.

Can you pick me up from Frankfurt Airport?

Yes. Send your flight number, expected arrival time, passenger count and luggage. We coordinate meeting point and pickup time directly with you.

How does flight monitoring work?

We check the flight number you provide and take changes to the actual arrival into account. Flight monitoring is included for airport pickups.

Is the price fixed in advance?

Yes. Website prices are starting prices. Your specific ride is confirmed with a fixed price before booking.

Are early-morning and late-night rides possible?

Yes. Transfers can generally be requested 24/7, subject to availability.
